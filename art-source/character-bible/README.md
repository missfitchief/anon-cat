# Locked character direction

Production-selected original ANON CAT, derived from supplied artwork. `front-master.png` controls the final face, proportions, colors and matte surface treatment; `front-transparent-master.png` is its alpha extraction. `turnaround-locked.png` supplies consistent front/side/back design references. This is a production selection, not a claim of separate user visual approval.

Supplied hierarchy: SlDNF.jpg upright front controls primary face, clothing and palette; qRDG5.jpg upright turnaround controls silhouette. pOPdn.jpg informs collar and low-back tail attachment; its different waist treatment is not imported. Gxxaj.jpg/oNmbm.jpg quadrupeds are secondary pose inspiration, never mixed into upright anatomy. No quadruped animation or morph is used.

Palette: orange #FF861C, knit gray #73767A (renders lighter under studio illumination), charcoal #282B2E, near-black #111315, warm paper #F2EFE8. Gray is fitted ribbed knit across mask, collar, body and tail sleeve. Orange ears, eye openings, muzzle, feline paws and tail end are underlying matte smooth cat surfaces, never gray fur or orange knit. No separate accessories, weapons or logos.

Face: broad feline head, raised fitted oval eye openings, white eyes with large rounded brown irises, dark central pupils and glossy highlights, small brown triangular nose, closed confident muzzle smile, three dark slender whiskers per cheek. Subtle half-lids communicate maturity and composure; relaxed lids partially cover full-sized irises without changing their design. Large head is retained from supplied mascot, with a lean rounded upright body rather than infant proportions. Collar rings the neck; sleeve cuffs meet paws; long tail attaches low at back.

Expression/pose assets: three coherent portraits side-eye, peek and relaxed; step-master.png upright rightward step with tail trailing left; peek-master.png tilted head and single paw at an edge; seated-master.png calm upright seated pose. Do not morph between these still geometries on-screen. Occlude pose swaps behind architecture and use the same grounded scale. Knit is baked into raster assets and will not swim in independent animation. Avoid distorting face or rib spacing.

Relaxed-eye correction: `../portraits/relaxed-master.png` is the corrected 1254 × 1254 master, with the original archived as `../portraits/relaxed-original-master.png`. It restores hero/side-eye iris and pupil proportions behind the relaxed eyelids. `expression-sheet.png` remains historical and is superseded as a relaxed-eye identity reference. Provenance is recorded in `../portraits/eye-correction.json`.

Animation-ready front has relaxed arms slightly clear of torso, feet apart on ground, ears/whiskers/tail fully framed. Dedicated peek is cropped by an implied vertical edge and should be composited against a wall. Use head-local motion sparingly; no whole-body floating. Separate raster materials do not represent an editable 3D cat rig.
